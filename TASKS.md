### Phase 5.5 — App-wide re-theme (what "the rest of the signed-in app" above involved)
- [x] **Re-themed globally, not scoped to one page**: since Phase 2/3 already built the whole app token-first (`client/src/index.css`'s `@theme` block feeding `bg-canvas`/`text-fg`/`bg-brand-500`/`border-border`/etc. utilities everywhere), re-theming the entire signed-in app to the warm palette was a **values-only edit** to that same block, not a per-component rewrite — `--color-canvas/surface/elevated` → near-white/white, `--color-border(-strong)` → warm greys, `--color-brand-400/500/600` → black/charcoal (now the primary action color, not a blue-violet accent), `--color-ai-500/600` → the handoff's electric blue-violet (AI/scan accents only), `--color-success/warning/danger` → the handoff's exact hexes, `--color-fg/-muted/-subtle` → near-black/warm-grey text. Added `--font-display` (Hanken Grotesk) and `--font-mono` (JetBrains Mono, overriding Tailwind's default mono stack) plus a small `--heading-md` etc. type-scale in a plain `:root` block. Both new font packages are imported once, globally, in `index.css` (landing.css no longer duplicates them).
  - **One correctness catch from this**: `brand-500` used to mean "the dark canvas' most saturated accent," safe to sit under the app's general near-white text (`text-fg`). Now it means "solid black," so anywhere text sits *inside* a brand-colored solid surface (the primary `Button` variant, the sidebar's active-nav pill, `MobileSidebar`'s active link) needed its `text-fg` changed to explicit `text-white` — `text-fg` itself stayed correct everywhere else (body text on the now-light canvas).
